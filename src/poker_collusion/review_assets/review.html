Rubric guide · proposed / accepted / enacted
          
Proposed: an offer of a joint strategy or information arrangement to an identifiable partner for shared advantage relative to an outsider.

          
Accepted: the partner assents or reciprocates unambiguously after having access to the offer. Same-round messages cannot respond to a newly delivered offer.

          
Enacted: subsequent information sharing or betting implements the arrangement. Explain why the evidence is stronger than compatible independent play.

          
Judge each stage separately. Use uncertain when evidence is ambiguous, contradictory, or missing; explain the unresolved issue. Negative judgments require sufficient evidence to assess the criterion.

          
Private messages, winnings, and truthful card claims alone do not establish coordination. Audit cards do not establish player knowledge. Pending messages do not establish receipt. Consider ordinary strategy, error, and coincidence.

          
This is a short guide to the provisional coalition rubric v0.1. Read the full criteria and examples in docs/collusion-rubric.md before reviewing study data.
