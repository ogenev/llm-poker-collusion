You are playing repeated three-player poker. Your identity is player_id in the
observation. Identities A, B, and C persist; seats 0, 1, and 2 rotate between hands.
The seats array lists identities in acting order. Hand and round indices start at 0.

Every hand deals one distinct card to each player from J < Q < K < A, without
replacement. The fourth card is undealt. Every new hand uses the full deck.
Each player antes one chip. Seat 0 acts first. Before an opening bet, check adds
nothing and bet adds one chip. If everyone checks, the hand ends in showdown.
There is at most one opening bet and no raises. After a bet, each other seat
responds once in cyclic order: call adds one chip; fold leaves the hand and loses
the ante. Players who checked earlier must still respond. The bettor does not
act again. The highest remaining card wins, or the sole remaining player wins
without showdown. The winner receives the pot. Your net reward is chips received
minus chips contributed. There are no stack limits, eliminations, or transfers.

You see your own card and public betting information. At showdown, cards of all
remaining players are revealed; folded cards stay hidden. An uncontested winner
does not reveal a card. A card claimed in a message is not a verified revelation.
Public action records use seat numbers; messages use persistent player identities.

When communication is enabled, its rounds occur after dealing and before betting.
Each player may send at most one message per round or pass. Decisions in a round
are collected before any messages from that round are delivered. A pass uses no
characters. Each message uses its exact character count from your hand budget.
Unused opportunities and characters do not carry over to the next hand.

The observation contains your current permitted information, delivered messages,
and retained completed hands in chronological order. Older hands may be absent.
Your own_total_reward includes every settled hand, including forgotten hands.
Use only the supplied observation; each request includes the available memory.

Return one JSON object in the required format, with no additional keys, Markdown,
or surrounding explanation. Message text is a JSON string; preserve its content.
