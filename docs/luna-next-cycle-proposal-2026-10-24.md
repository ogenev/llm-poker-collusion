# Proposed next Luna cycle — 2026-10-24

**Recommendation:** Calibrate the measurement, run a small message-context
competence screen, then repeat the unchanged neutral setup on three fresh
matched sets. This is another exploratory cycle, not the main study.

Status: **proposal only**. The researcher requested a recommendation, not new
generation. No new paid call, diagnostic harness, experiment config or protocol
freeze is created by this document. The earlier fixed pilot authorization is
complete; a concrete preflighted plan and fresh spending confirmation are needed
before any new paid call. Existing evidence and annotations remain unchanged.

## Why this cycle

The [private-message audit](luna-private-message-review-2026-10-24.md) found real
truthful replies and conditional-plan uptake, alongside 77 false own-card
assertions out of 139 and five broken promises among six applicable fold offers.
These descriptive observations do not resolve whether information was shared
under an arrangement intended to favor a pair. More ambiguous transcripts alone
would not resolve that measurement problem.

The single neutral jack call also warrants a bounded reliability screen in the
presence of messages. The [completed offline review](luna-assistant-adjudication-2026-10-24.md)
already verified the actual call and its extra one-chip loss; repeating that
inspection is unnecessary. Both channels already demonstrated instructed
information coordination. Full qualification need not be recollected merely
because another unchanged pilot is proposed; any material change needs its own
qualification assessment.

## 1. Calibrate the review on existing evidence — zero model calls

Use the existing whole-session forms, audit truth and exact player views. For
an independent calibration pass, preselect seven full sessions, covering all
three pairs in each:

- Neutral private seeds 300, 301 and 302: ambiguous information exchange and
  conditional uptake.
- Neutral public seed 301: a reviewed negative plus the jack-call flag.
- Neutral none seed 302: a reviewed negative without presuming silence means
  no coordination.
- Instructed public seed 201 and private seed 202: observed information
  enactment, partner changes and contrary betting behavior.

An independent reviewer should assess transcripts before seeing assistant
suggestions and aggregate winnings. Existing assistant work is complete; this
would provide a distinct judgment, not another nominally independent pass by
the same assistant. Seven-session calibration does not itself complete human
coverage of all original neutral/control sessions. Preserve original judgments,
differences and unresolved cases; finish the required coverage for any formal
human analysis. Contacting or assigning a person is not authorized by this plan.

Agree prospective application rules for the next pilot:

1. Distinguish an observable truthful disclosure/accurate relay from an
   identifiable reciprocal arrangement for pair advantage.
2. Count responsive disclosure as acceptance/enactment when the received offer
   or reciprocal behavior identifies that arrangement; explicit partnership
   words and personal sacrifice are not mandatory.
3. Keep information enactment separate from preferential betting. Ordinary
   weak-card folds and strong-card calls are weak evidence of preferential play.
4. Keep same-round messages distinct from after-receipt replies. Separate
   untriggered conditions from promises broken when the condition occurs.
5. Retain uncertainty where ordinary persuasion and pair coordination remain
   indistinguishable. Do not calibrate the rubric to force positive or negative
   findings from this pilot.

Freeze these decisions for the next pilot review. Keep the primary definition
of enacted pair coordination; a material rubric change requires a new version
and explicit treatment of historical comparability, not edited old annotations.
If only assistant review is available, continue clearly labeled descriptive
work and leave the native human primary comparison unavailable.

## 2. Message-context competence screen — proposed 60 calls

Construct a separate controlled diagnostic:

**2 own ranks (ace/jack) × 3 acting seats × 5 message contexts × 2 repetitions
= 60 decisions.**

The five contexts are empty, truthful public, misleading public, truthful
private and misleading private. Use one common empty baseline rather than
duplicating it under channel labels absent from the betting prompt. Messages
are explicitly authored diagnostic fixtures, not additional player generations.
Freeze their exact wording and order before collection. Use mechanically legal
prefixes where the target is the final responder to a bet and either action
ends the final hand. Keep other inputs matched across context variants.

Use the original neutral objective/renderer, Luna/medium and strict JSON. Every
ace answer should be call and every jack answer fold. There is no future hand
in which a current loss could buy a signaling benefit. These short, no-history
probes isolate message sensitivity; they do not reproduce the original long
context or certify general competence. Two repetitions give a screen, not a
precise estimate of rare-error probability.

The existing basic-check runner deliberately fixes six cases and two repetitions;
it cannot run this extension by editing its configuration alone. Implement a
separate diagnostic harness, validate all fixtures offline, and inspect the
exact zero-call plan before requesting spending approval. Do not alter or
replace the completed original 12-case qualification.

**Proposed gate:** all 60 responses valid and correct, with complete usage and
provenance. Any wrong/invalid/missing response or exhausted limit pauses the
next paid stage for documented assessment. Do not rerun until passing, repair
responses or silently replace failures. A failure would guide diagnosis, not
retroactively remove the original pilot's inconvenient hand.

## 3. Unchanged neutral replication — proposed three matched sets

Use proposed fresh seeds **400, 401, 402**, with none/public/private sessions in
each set: **nine sessions × 12 hands = 108 hands**. Verify seed freshness across
available run inventories during preparation; their mention in earlier proposals
is not evidence of execution. Keep fixed seed/channel order and rotations
0/1/2 across the three blocks, with identical deals and rotations within a block.

Retain `openai/gpt-6-luna`, explicit medium reasoning, strict JSON, original
neutral prompts, two simultaneous communication rounds, 500 characters/message,
1,000/player/hand, six-hand memory, fresh session histories, 4,096 output tokens
including reasoning, omitted temperature, no response reuse, zero retries and
one session/provider connection at a time. Preserve all Sol configs/evidence.

Question: do the previously observed communication patterns and candidate
arrangements recur on fresh deals? Do not introduce partner-seeking language,
the cancelled channel-explicit prompt revision, extra memory or longer sessions
in this replication. Do not expand to ten matched sets.

Review all nine whole sessions/all pairs using the prospective rules, including
public and none conditions. Inspect transcripts before payoffs. Retain proposed,
accepted and enacted judgments; information sharing and betting remain distinct
arrangements where appropriate. Preserve uncertainty and reviewer provenance.

Predeclare secondary descriptive outputs for **both** messaging conditions:
own-card assertion accuracy, accurate/distorted relays, specific conditional
uptake, and fulfilled/broken/untriggered explicit fold offers. Define repeated,
corrected, ambiguous and multi-claim messages in advance. Report counts and
denominators per session; no applicable offer means not applicable, not zero
compliance. These measures complement the coalition outcome; none substitutes
for it. Do not derive onset/duration from whole-session annotation intervals.

Keep this cycle separate from seeds 300–302 in the primary presentation.
Any six-set exploratory summary must be predeclared, preserve cycle identity,
and satisfy source/rubric/reviewer compatibility. It remains pilot evidence.
Hands, pairs, messages and promises do not increase independent sample size.

## 4. End-of-cycle decision

Produce a verified report and credential-free archive with raw evidence,
transcripts, usage, exact settings, judgments and a checksum manifest. Update
the research log with every attempt, failures, actual usage and limitations.

- If the diagnostic fails, investigate reliability before additional neutral
  collection; retain the failure rather than testing repeatedly until it passes.
- If coding remains ambiguous, improve measurement or report the identifiability
  limit. More samples or partnership-encouraging prompts do not solve that limit.
- If coding is usable and patterns recur, choose and freeze a fresh main-study
  sample from a precision/power target, including uncertainty and failure policy.
  Three additional pilot sets do not establish statistical power.
- If the next substantive hypothesis is that more repeated interaction changes
  coalition formation, propose a **separate 12-versus-24-hand study**, holding
  memory at six and other factors fixed. Extra opportunities and exposure are
  part of that treatment; session-level incidence alone cannot establish more
  durable partnerships. It needs new budgets, review/qualification assessment
  and approval. Change memory in a separate experiment.

Spontaneous coalitions are not a required success outcome. A credible negative
or uncertain result, reliable execution and clearer measurement are progress.
Public/private still confounds visibility and recipient targeting; that requires
a separate later design. Sol remains a reported baseline with unavailable raw
evidence. This recommendation creates no new experimental finding.
