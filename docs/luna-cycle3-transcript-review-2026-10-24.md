## Coding and timing cautions

- A prediction such as public hand 6's “don't expect a call” is retained without
  inferring an explicit fold commitment. Requests that another player fold are
  distinct from promises by the sender to fold.
- Identical offers to different beneficiaries are separate directed offers.
  Repeats to the same beneficiary with the same trigger within a hand collapse.
  Both channels have one applicable resolved fold offer, broken; untriggered
  offers are excluded from that compliance denominator.
- A's private hand-6 false ace claim to C is not corrected by its true jack claim
  sent to B. Likewise, different claims sent to different recipients in hand 10
  remain separate information states. Public hand 7's ace-to-king revision reaches
  the same audience; both original and replacement assertions remain counted.
- Seventeen additional source-to-view links and the earlier live-prefix event
  hashes validate against the completed private journal. Availability establishes
  what could be known, not what caused an action.
- Six private candidate worksheets retain candidate implementations, narrow
  acceptance evidence, contrary/alternative events and censoring at hand 12.
  Confirmed coalition onset and last confirmed implementation remain null. No
  coalition onset is backdated to an isolated truthful disclosure.
